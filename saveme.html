“What did I spend my money on?”

Daily logging felt like homework, so people stopped doing it. SaveMe keeps the steps to log a spend to a minimum, then shows the habit back to you clearly enough to act on it.

Features

Four screens. One clear picture.

Everything sits one tap away on the bottom bar, and every number on screen is worked out on your phone.

01 · ADD

Log a spend in seconds

Type an amount or tap a quick chip, pick a category, save. The monthly budget line moves as soon as you do.

  • Quick amounts: +5, +10, +20, +50
  • Today, yesterday or any past date
  • Income gets its own tab, with sources

02 · HISTORY

Every ringgit, by day

A month at a glance, grouped by day with daily totals. One tap filters it down to a single category.

  • Move month by month
  • Tap to edit, swipe to delete, with undo
  • Spending and income side by side

03 · SAVINGS

What your savings are worth

Stocks, gold and EPF valued together from what you hold and what you paid, plus named savings jars with a target.

  • Gain since you put the money in
  • Savings jars (tabung) with progress
  • Remaining budget and a spending breakdown

04 · NEWS

News that matters to your money

Market and Malaysian finance stories, filtered for relevance and fetched inside a daily request budget.

  • Search headlines, filter by section
  • Bullish, bearish or neutral at a glance
  • Pull to refresh, swipe to dismiss

Demo

See it in motion

A 17-second loop: log RM 22.50 for food, find it in History, then check Savings and News.

Under the hood

Built carefully, not just quickly

A native Android app written in Java, structured so the screens, the data and the money maths each stay in their own lane.

Encrypted on device

Records live in a Room database encrypted with SQLCipher. The key is held by the Android Keystore, and biometrics can unlock sign-in.

Budget-aware feeds

Prices and news come from Alpha Vantage and RSS. A refresh policy spends the free tier’s 25 daily requests where they matter most.

Accessible by default

Text is designed for 7:1 contrast, every tap target is at least 48 dp, and every progress bar has a spoken description.

Modern Android

Targets Android 16 with edge-to-edge layouts, MVVM with a repository layer, ViewBinding, and the Navigation component.

  • Java 17
  • Room + SQLCipher
  • Firebase Auth
  • Google Sign-In
  • AndroidX Biometric
  • Material 3
  • MPAndroidChart
  • Alpha Vantage + RSS
